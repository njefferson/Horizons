          Bringing a copy back

          Moving to a new device, or restoring after something went wrong. Choose an exported file and Quietkeep will tell you what is in it before anything changes.

          Take in what I don’t have adds anything this copy has and this device doesn’t, and removes nothing — that is the one for carrying your work between your own devices. Replace everything throws away what is here and uses the copy instead — that is the one for a device you are setting up again.

          Reminders that reach you

          Quietkeep can only bring something back while it is open. Send what you are holding to your calendar and the calendar will tell you instead — it already runs when this app does not.
