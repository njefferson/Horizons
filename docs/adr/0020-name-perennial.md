# ADR-0020 · The name is Perennial

**Status:** **Superseded by [ADR-0021](0021-name-reopened.md)** — Perennial withdrawn
2026-07-28 · **Date:** 2026-07-28
**Supersedes:** [ADR-0018](0018-name-and-slug.md)

> **This record was wrong, and the reason is worth more than the record.** Perennial is
> held by three software companies and `perennial.pages.dev` is taken. It was recommended
> on two searches that asked *"is another planner called this?"* instead of *"is this name
> taken in software?"* — see [ADR-0021](0021-name-reopened.md).
>
> **The graveyard below is still current and still authoritative.** Perennial has been
> appended to it.

## Decision

The app is **Perennial**. Tagline and epigraph are unchanged: *"Out of sight. Never out
of mind."* / *"It holds the rest, so you can rest."*

**It is not cleared.** No USPTO knockout has been run in classes 9 or 42, and no direct
store searches. See [V-04](../verifications.md). Provisional here means *decided well
enough to build on and write down*, not *safe to print on anything permanent*.

## Why

Every optics candidate described **seeing**. That was the wrong target. What this app
uniquely claims is **return** — nothing goes silent, everything comes back whether or not
you remember to look. Perennial names that directly:

- **It comes back on its own, untended.** Not because it was kept alive by effort. That is
  the return engine (law 1) and the reason resurfacing had to be structural rather than
  habitual.
- **"Ready again," not overdue** (law 5). A perennial has a season; between seasons it is
  not late.
- **A dormant perennial has not failed** (law 8). Rest is legitimate, and the word already
  knows it — dormancy is part of the organism's normal year, not a lapse from it.

It also collides with nothing Noah owns, which the previous name did.

**The floral reading is a branding choice, not a property of the word.** A *perennial
stream* flows year-round where an intermittent one does not; a *perennial problem* and a
*perennial favourite* are simply recurring. The brand leans to periodicity and endurance.
Nothing about borders, bedding plants, or bees.

**The pattern, worth keeping:** single evocative words in class 9 are effectively
exhausted. What survives is compounds, coinages, and slightly-odd words. Wynts tested the
coinage branch and died on sound; **Quietkeep** is the compound branch, and it cleared —
see [ADR-0024](0024-name-quietkeep.md).

## Consequences

- **Nothing in the schema, the event vocabulary, or the file formats encodes the product
  name.** That was true under the old name and stays true — it is why this rename cost a
  copy pass and no refactor, and why a failed clearance would cost only another one.
- **"Horizons" survives as domain vocabulary and that is deliberate.** Product law 4 —
  *higher horizons project lineage and health downward* — and the *horizon-integrity
  engine* keep the word. It is standard planning terminology, it is not user-facing
  branding, and the sibling app owning the literal sense creates no confusion between two
  products. A CI check asserts these survived, because a careless global replace would
  silently destroy an invariant's own statement.
- `LICENSE.md`'s Required Notice URL still points at `njefferson/Horizons` **on purpose**.
  It moves in the same commit as the GitHub repo rename; changing it earlier would aim the
  notice at a 404.
- The clearance is owed before any public release copy: USPTO classes 9 and 42, App Store
  and Play direct searches, `perennial.pages.dev`.

## What would overturn it

A USPTO knockout returning a live mark in class 9 or 42. Nothing else — the floral reading
is answerable, and Noah has accepted it.
